About me

Most of my work is agentic RAG and complex LLM systems — retrieval pipelines, agents that call real tools, and the classical ML and NLP that still beat a prompt for clustering, ranking and extraction. Neural search with RAG and agents over unstructured medical data at Sorcero, an LLM-driven ticketing pipeline at Tekever, computer vision at scale before that. Lately that has extended into production voice agents too.

What I actually care about is the unglamorous half — the evals, guardrails and infrastructure that decide whether a demo survives contact with real users. I've shipped that half at a startup, a scale-up and a consultancy, and I like the teams I work with to be better for my having been there.

Away from the keyboard I'm playing music or outdoors — bungee jumping, climbing, paddle boarding. I also shoot a lot of photography, which lives on a separate Instagram.
